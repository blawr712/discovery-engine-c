# v0.3 Intelligence Specification

**Status:** Released as v0.3.0 on 2026-08-09. See `V0.3_RELEASE.md` for the
validated acceptance record.

## Objective

Improve the quality and research usefulness of Discovery Engine candidates
without sacrificing complete supported-market coverage or deterministic,
explainable scoring.

## Principles

1. Deterministic factors produce the Discovery Score.
2. AI research never changes the numerical score.
3. Every exclusion, factor, penalty, and missing value is explainable.
4. Ambiguous metadata lowers confidence; it does not silently remove a company.
5. Canadian and U.S. proportions reflect eligible listings, not quotas.
6. New scoring behavior must be comparable with the validated v0.2 baseline.

## Sprint 1: Universe Quality and Scoring Foundation

- Classify operating equities and structural vehicles from provider metadata.
- Exclude obvious SPACs, shell companies, funds, warrants, rights, units, and
  preferred securities through configuration-driven rules.
- Retain unknown classifications with an explicit confidence signal.
- Introduce a common factor-result model containing raw value, points,
  maximum points, availability, and explanation.
- Add score confidence and a machine-readable factor breakdown to reports.
- Preserve v0.2 score values while the framework is introduced.
- Collect provider fundamentals through a versioned metadata schema.
- Calculate growth, profitability, cash-flow, and balance-sheet shadow factors
  before integrating them into the Discovery Score.
- Export curated candidates and factor-coverage analytics.
- Support deterministic balanced validation samples across Canada and the U.S.

## Planned Sprint 2: Financial Intelligence

- Add growth, profitability, free-cash-flow, balance-sheet, valuation, and
  risk factors.
- Define missing-data and stale-data policies for every factor.
- Rebalance weights only after distribution and candidate-review analysis.

### Increment 1: Valuation, Risk, and Data Quality

- Add shadow earnings-yield, sales-yield, and enterprise-value-to-EBITDA
  valuation factors.
- Add shadow liquidity, leverage, and cash-flow-to-earnings quality factors.
- Classify factor inputs as fresh, undated, missing, invalid, or stale.
- Exclude stale and invalid values from scoring rather than treating them as
  weak financial performance.
- Discount confidence for usable but undated provider fundamentals.
- Keep the official Discovery Score and its ranking unchanged.

### Increment 2: Validation and Calibration

- Export technical, country-relative, sector-relative, and fundamental
  percentiles for every successful candidate.
- Measure technical/fundamental rank correlation and top-candidate overlap.
- Surface the largest rank disagreements, low-confidence candidates, factor
  distributions, and implausible provider-value outliers.
- Calculate configurable experimental blend scenarios using comparable
  percentile ranks.
- Preserve official Discovery Score order in every calibration export and
  prevent experimental scenarios from controlling candidate ranking.

### Increment 3: Calibration Hardening

- Apply configurable `cap` or `invalid` treatment to extreme provider inputs
  while preserving the original source value for audit.
- Mark sector-inappropriate factors as not applicable and remove their weight
  from both normalized scoring and confidence calculations.
- Confidence-adjust fundamental scores and require minimum confidence before a
  candidate can enter a blended experimental scenario.
- Add sector-relative fundamental percentiles and explicit blend-ineligibility
  explanations.
- Report top-20, top-50, and top-100 overlap plus scenario promotions and
  demotions.
- Classify factor readiness as `ready`, `limited`, or `shadow_only` from
  applicable-data coverage.
- Keep all hardened fundamentals and blends in the non-authoritative shadow
  lane.

### Increment 4: Coverage-Neutral Blending and Acceptance Gates

- Select a core fundamental lane dynamically from factors meeting the minimum
  coverage threshold in both Canada and the United States.
- Calculate country-relative or country/sector-relative fundamental
  percentiles, falling back to the country peer group when a sector sample is
  too small.
- Scale low-confidence peer percentiles continuously toward neutral instead of
  applying a hard eligibility cutoff.
- Let technical scoring define a fixed top-100 candidate pool and allow
  fundamentals to rerank only within 25-company technical bands.
- Explain candidate-pool membership separately from fundamental-data
  eligibility.
- Measure country eligibility disparity, top-list retention, and maximum rank
  movement against configurable acceptance gates.
- Emit an automatic pass/fail decision with reasons for every experimental
  scenario.

## Planned Sprint 3: Candidate Reporting

- Export a ranked research-candidate report separately from diagnostic rows.
- Include rank, percentile, strongest signals, risks, factor coverage, and
  score explanations.

### Increment 1: Decision-Ready Scenario Reports

- Rebuild calibration and research artifacts from a completed run without
  initializing market-data providers or downloading data.
- Validate saved run identifiers, completion state, and checkpoint
  completeness before recalibration.
- Export one experimental research queue for each passing weighted scenario.
- Include official and experimental rank, movement, technical score, core
  fundamentals, confidence, peer percentile, and peer group.
- Explain promotions, demotions, core strengths, core weaknesses, missing or
  treated data, and outlier flags.
- Export a combined top-25-per-scenario human-review queue and scenario
  country/sector composition summary.
- Record recalibration provenance and generated artifacts in the source run
  manifest.

### Increment 2: Scenario Selection and v0.3 Research Queue

- Compare every passing weighted scenario side by side across the fixed
  technical candidate pool.
- Calculate consensus rank, cross-scenario rank range, and stable versus
  weight-sensitive classifications.
- Measure candidate-set agreement at the top 10, 25, 50, and 100.
- Recommend the passing scenario with the highest technical weight and honor a
  configured selection only while it continues to pass acceptance gates.
- Export a formal decision record explaining the recommendation and confirming
  that the official Discovery Score remains unchanged.
- Export one selected `v0.3_research_candidates` queue with consensus and
  sensitivity fields for downstream research.

## Planned Sprint 4: AI Research

- Generate optional, cached, source-backed summaries only for top deterministic
  candidates.
- Cover business overview, growth drivers, risks, recent developments, and
  unanswered research questions.

### Increment 1: Deterministic Research Packets

- Build research packets offline from the configured passing v0.3 research
  queue without initializing market-data or AI providers.
- Limit research to an explicit top-N selection and preserve selected and
  official ranks exactly as recorded by calibration.
- Include company identity, technical signals, core fundamental evidence,
  peer comparison, data-quality notes, and deterministic research questions.
- Define a pluggable research-provider boundary and a versioned prompt that
  forbids score or rank changes and requires citations for sourced claims.
- Cache provider responses by prompt version and packet fingerprint.
- Isolate provider failures per company and reuse valid cached responses.
- Export machine-readable JSON and human-readable Markdown packets.
- Keep AI synthesis disabled by default and record packet provenance in the
  saved run manifest.

### Increment 2: Source-Backed Evidence

- Collect sources only through explicit `--collect-sources` or reviewed local
  manifest options; packet-only research remains offline.
- Retrieve recent primary SEC filings with a declared contact user agent and
  conservative publisher pacing.
- Cache raw responses with freshness limits and hash every attached document.
- Record URL, title, publisher, publication/retrieval dates, source category,
  quality priority, and content hash in an evidence manifest.
- Enforce HTTPS domain allowlists, deduplicate evidence, reject stale or
  malformed citations, and isolate source failures by company and provider.
- Distinguish computed fields, sourced evidence, and future AI interpretation.
- Keep AI disabled and prevent source collection or synthesis from changing
  official or selected scores and ranks.

### Increment 3: Validated Cited Synthesis

- Add an explicit `--synthesize` action requiring attached evidence, a local
  API key, and the configuration safety switch.
- Send bounded normalized source excerpts through the OpenAI Responses API
  with response storage disabled and a strict JSON output schema.
- Separate sourced facts from interpretation and require every sourced fact to
  cite an attached URL and matching SHA-256 content hash.
- Reject unsupported citations, uncited facts, malformed claim classes,
  refusals, ranking fields, recommendation fields, and invalid output shapes.
- Treat filing contents as untrusted data and forbid document instructions
  from changing synthesis behavior.
- Cache validated responses by packet, evidence, prompt, provider, and model.
- Skip candidates without evidence and isolate API or validation failures by
  company.
- Export JSON provenance, validation counts, cache use, and readable Markdown
  briefs while preserving every official and selected rank and score.

### Increment 4: Human Review and Acceptance

- Audit saved research artifacts entirely offline without market-data, source,
  or AI provider initialization.
- Revalidate every synthesis and measure candidate evidence coverage, country
  coverage, synthesis completion, citation coverage, section completeness,
  sourced-evidence use, failures, and ranking integrity.
- Apply configurable automated pass/fail gates and preserve unevaluated status
  when synthesis has not run.
- Export a claim-level human-review CSV with source URLs and hashes plus fields
  for factual accuracy, citation support, materiality notes, and disposition.
- Prevent automated checks from granting human approval; a passing audit may
  advance only to `pending_human_review`.
- Finalize a completed review as approved, rejected, or incomplete and record
  the decision and source artifacts in the saved run manifest.
- Refuse final approval when automated gates fail or any review row is pending,
  unsupported, inaccurate, or rejected.

### Sprint 5: Batch Release Validation

- Report provider token usage for uncached synthesis calls so batch cost can be
  observed without estimating from candidate counts.
- Export a candidate-level automated audit alongside the claim review queue,
  including evidence, synthesis, cache, validation, and section coverage.
- Produce a consolidated candidate release report after human review with
  approved, rejected, incomplete, and not-ready dispositions.
- Preserve successful candidate outputs when another candidate lacks evidence
  or fails synthesis, while preventing incomplete batches from silently
  receiving release approval.
- Validate the complete workflow on a varied multi-candidate batch before the
  v0.3 release decision.
- Assign deterministic claim risk from classification, section, numerical
  content, material terms, and citation complexity.
- Require review of all high-risk claims, reproducibly sample lower-risk
  claims, and preserve at least one reviewed claim per populated section.
- Bind finalization to stable sampled claim IDs so deleted, substituted, or
  duplicated queue rows cannot receive approval.
- Support balanced ranked research selection without changing global scenario
  ranks.
- Route U.S. evidence to SEC EDGAR and Canadian evidence to explicitly curated,
  allowlisted issuer-primary URLs; do not automate SEDAR+ access.
- Cache Canadian source content, compute and optionally pin SHA-256 hashes, and
  expose candidate-level provider provenance.
- Keep synthesis cache identity stable when only evidence retrieval timestamps
  change, while invalidating it whenever source content hashes change.

## v0.3 Acceptance Criteria

- [x] Structural false positives are materially reduced in the top 100.
- [x] Scores remain deterministic and fully decomposable into factors.
- [x] Missing data is visible through factor availability and score confidence.
- [x] A full-universe run completes with zero pipeline errors.
- [x] AI output is optional and cannot alter ranking.
