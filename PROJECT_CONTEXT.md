# Project Context

## Mission

Reduce thousands of North American public companies into a focused list of high-quality research candidates.

## Investment Philosophy

Discovery Engine is not a portfolio manager and does not make investment decisions. It identifies companies worthy of deeper human research.

## Universe

Canada
- TSX
- TSXV
- Future: CSE

United States
- NASDAQ
- NYSE
- AMEX

Coverage policy: include every supported listing that meets the configured
eligibility rules. Country proportions reflect the available market universe;
they are descriptive, not sampling quotas.

## Preferred Market Cap

Preferred: $50M-$750M

Allowed: $10M-$1B

## Engineering Principles

1. Configuration over hardcoding.
2. One responsibility per module.
3. Data collection is separate from scoring.
4. Every score should be explainable.
5. Architecture before features.
6. Documentation evolves with the software.

## Core Terminology

Universe, Signal, Factor, Discovery Score, Research Candidate, Engine, Pre-Filter.

## Long-Term Vision

Process the entire North American market weekly using a modular, provider-agnostic architecture with historical tracking and AI-assisted research summaries.
