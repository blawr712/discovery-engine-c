# v0.4 Platform Specification

## Objective

Turn validated file-backed runs into a queryable local platform for historical
comparison, change detection, and future dashboards without coupling storage
to market-data collection or scoring.

## Sprint 1: Historical Data Foundation

### Increment 1: Offline Run Index

- Index only completed runs through the existing validated checkpoint loader.
- Store manifests and ordered per-company results in a versioned SQLite schema.
- Make repeated indexing idempotent and transactional.
- Preserve complete JSON payloads while exposing common query columns.
- Keep run directories and exports as the source of truth.
- Initialize no market-data, evidence, or AI provider during history actions.
- Provide an offline database summary for operational verification.

### Increment 2: Run Comparison and Change Detection

- Compare any two indexed runs entirely offline.
- Classify universe entrants, exits, retained equities, status transitions,
  successful-candidate rank changes, score changes, and data-quality changes.
- Report the ten largest rank gains and declines.
- Summarize status-transition and country/sector composition changes.
- Export deterministic JSON and review-ready CSV artifacts.

### Increment 3: Candidate Timelines and Weekly Change Reports

- Export chronological ticker status, candidate-rank, score, confidence, and
  fundamental data-quality timelines across every indexed appearance.
- Select the latest pair of complete indexed runs with matching universe sizes.
- Identify promotions into and demotions from the successful candidate set.
- Flag fingerprint differences so configuration changes are not mistaken for
  purely market-driven movement.
- Export a readable Markdown briefing with detailed JSON and CSV artifacts.

### Increment 4: Read-Only Local Dashboard

- Serve an offline dashboard exclusively on the loopback interface.
- Open the SQLite history database in enforced read-only mode.
- Explore indexed runs and filter candidates by status, country, ticker, and
  company name without initializing market-data or AI providers.
- Inspect per-ticker timelines and the latest comparable weekly movement report.
- Use a self-contained responsive interface with no external web dependencies.

## Sprint 2: Candidate Detail and Explainability

### Increment 1: Score Explanations and Relative Descriptors

- Define Discovery and Fundamental Scores in plain language without presenting
  either as a return forecast or recommendation.
- Classify successful candidates using deterministic within-run percentiles.
- Describe technical and fundamental confidence separately as data coverage.
- Display stored factor points, maximums, explanations, applicability, and
  data-quality treatments in the candidate-detail view.
- Preserve filtered and failed status reasons alongside indexed score history.

### Increment 2: Durable Price Performance and Charting

- Compress and persist provenance-compatible one-year adjusted-close and volume
  snapshots for successful candidates and their country benchmarks.
- Reject cache artifacts newer than the completed run to prevent historical
  look-ahead contamination.
- Calculate 1-week, 1-month, 3-month, 6-month, and 1-year percentage returns
  with benchmark-relative differences.
- Display interactive normalized ticker-versus-benchmark price charts entirely
  offline in candidate details.
- Default charts to actual adjusted close with a separate percentage-comparison
  mode, date axes, range controls, and hover values.
- Detect possible split/corporate-action discontinuities and suppress affected
  period returns pending adjustment verification.
- Make the dashboard dark mode by default with no external visual dependencies.

### Increment 3: Corporate-Action-Aware Price Integrity

- Request and preserve Yahoo split and dividend events with daily price history.
- Verify whether a declared split mathematically explains a price discontinuity.
- Adjust pre-event prices and volume only for verified unadjusted splits, while
  preventing double adjustment of already normalized histories.
- Classify chart data as clean, verified, verified-adjusted, or unresolved.
- Add price-quality labels, split markers, volume bars, and snapshot coverage
  analytics to the dashboard.

## Sprint 3: Candidate Workflows

### Increment 1: Side-by-Side Candidate Comparison

- Compare two to five unique tickers from the same indexed run.
- Align Discovery and Fundamental Scores, percentiles, ranks, confidence, and
  price-data quality without presenting a recommendation.
- Compare available period returns and normalized price histories.
- Display technical and fundamental factor strengths in a common heatmap.
- Keep comparison bounded, deterministic, read-only, and entirely offline.

### Increment 2: Persistent Watchlists and Change Tracking

- Create and manage bounded named watchlists from the local dashboard.
- Persist watchlists atomically in a separate local JSON file while keeping the
  indexed history database strictly read-only.
- Add tickers directly or from the candidate explorer and reject symbols that
  have never appeared in indexed history.
- Compare tracked status, candidate rank, and Discovery Score with the
  immediately preceding indexed run.
- Keep all watchlist actions explicit, loopback-only, and independent of market
  data or AI providers.

### Increment 3: Candidate Research Terminal

- Reframe the dashboard as a compact fintech research terminal with persistent
  workflow navigation and a global run context.
- Summarize why each candidate ranks, what limits its score, how it compares
  with the successful-candidate median, and where source data is incomplete.
- Expose deterministic technical-score drivers and unused factor capacity
  without presenting either as a recommendation or expected return.
- Add YTD returns, latest-price context, 20/50/200-session moving averages,
  chart zoom, volume, corporate-action markers, and crosshair inspection.
- Keep actual adjusted-close and benchmark-relative views clearly separated,
  with SPY for U.S. equities and XIU.TO for Canadian equities.
- Preserve the local, self-contained, offline, read-only market-data boundary.

### Increment 4: OHLCV and Advanced Charting

- Preserve adjusted daily open, high, low, close, and volume values from the
  existing provider cache when completed runs are indexed.
- Apply verified split normalization consistently across every OHLC price and
  volume field without double-adjusting provider-normalized histories.
- Add line and candlestick views, color-coded volume, complete OHLCV hover
  inspection, and linear or logarithmic price axes.
- Add optional SMA 20/50/200, EMA 20, and 20-session Bollinger Band overlays.
- Report OHLCV coverage explicitly and keep older close-only snapshots usable
  through a deterministic line-chart fallback.
- Keep all charting self-contained and historical; no dashboard interaction may
  request live market data or imply a forecast.

## Sprint 4: Moonshot Discovery

### Increment 1: Offline Universe and Shadow Scoring

- Establish a separate $2 million–$50 million operating-equity lane without
  weakening the official Discovery Engine market-cap policy.
- Label sub-$10 million issuers as nano-cap and the remaining eligible issuers
  as micro-cap for transparent cohort analysis.
- Calculate independent Upside Potential and Risk of Ruin scores rather than
  hiding risk inside a single optimistic composite.
- Gate classifications using data confidence and make stale, invalid, and
  missing fundamental evidence explicit.
- Export deterministic candidate CSV and full analysis JSON artifacts from any
  completed run without provider or AI calls.
- Record model version, source-run provenance, artifact paths, and the unchanged
  status of official scores and ranks in the run manifest.
- Treat liquidity, volatility/drawdown, dilution, and reverse-split history as
  pending requirements for later increments, not as inferred or neutral data.

### Increment 2: Market Risk and Trading Viability

- Derive 30-session average dollar volume, annualized daily volatility, and
  one-year maximum drawdown from adjusted price history.
- Collect up to 18 months of reported shares outstanding and calculate dilution
  only when observations span at least 180 days.
- Count reported reverse splits independently from unverified price jumps.
- Reuse only price caches no newer than the completed source run during the
  default offline analysis.
- Provide an explicit, optionally bounded live collection action for missing
  price and share history using existing cache, retry, pacing, cooldown, and
  circuit-breaker controls.
- Prevent watch and priority classifications until market-risk factor coverage
  passes a configurable gate; label low-coverage risk bands as unresolved.
- Persist reusable market-evidence provenance, per-company errors, coverage, and
  collection status without changing official Discovery scores or ranks.
- Report capture ranges and withhold cross-section comparability when evidence
  falls below the configured 99% threshold or is collected more than seven days
  apart; always identify the residual excluded cohort.

### Increment 3: Calibration, Validation, and Forward Baseline

- Reproduce the baseline Moonshot scores and ranks from stored factor evidence
  before accepting any calibration output.
- Stress-test growth-emphasis, survival-first, and trading-viability weighting
  scenarios without changing official Moonshot or Discovery ranks.
- Measure top-10, top-25, and top-50 overlap plus per-candidate rank sensitivity.
- Report factor distributions, risk outliers, and selection rates by country,
  size tier, and sector.
- Flag material country and size-tier selection-rate dispersion for review
  without forcing balanced representation or treating population share as bias.
- Enforce hard integrity gates for synchronized evidence, baseline
  reproducibility, resolved Priority risk, and maximum zero-liquidity penalty.
- Export deterministic scenario, candidate-validation, and analysis artifacts
  from an offline command.
- Freeze every comparable candidate, including control cohorts, in an immutable
  forward-test baseline with fixed 1M, 3M, 6M, and 1Y eligibility dates.
- Treat structural calibration as model validation rather than evidence of
  future performance or an investment recommendation.

### Increment 4: Moonshot Research Terminal

- Load deterministic Moonshot analysis, calibration, and forward-baseline
  artifacts through bounded read-only dashboard APIs.
- Filter the complete cohort by classification, country, size tier, ticker,
  and company name while defaulting to actionable research selections.
- Present Upside Potential, Risk of Ruin, overall evidence confidence, and
  market-risk confidence as distinct concepts.
- Explain factor contributions, strongest upside evidence, largest risk,
  missing inputs, and material liquidity, volatility, drawdown, dilution, and
  reverse-split warnings.
- Display per-candidate ranks and classifications under every configured stress
  scenario without changing the official Moonshot ordering.
- Surface automated validation gates, top-list scenario overlap, and country,
  size-tier, and sector selection-rate diagnostics without imposing quotas.
- Display the immutable forward cohort, benchmark, tracking status, and fixed
  1M, 3M, 6M, and 1Y eligibility dates; keep outcomes pending until eligible.
- Keep the terminal self-contained and offline: browsing and filtering saved
  artifacts must never initialize a market-data or AI provider.
