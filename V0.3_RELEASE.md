## Safety and Scope

- AI output cannot modify scores, ranks, or candidate eligibility.
- Every research claim requires a citation matching attached evidence by URL
  and SHA-256 content hash.
- Final approval always requires a human decision; automation cannot approve
  its own output.
- Review queues are bound to stable claim IDs and reject missing, duplicated,
  or substituted rows.
- SEC collection is restricted to U.S. candidates.
- Canadian automation uses explicitly curated, allowlisted issuer-primary HTML
  URLs. SEDAR+ is not scraped or accessed automatically.
- Fundamental factors remain explainable and confidence-aware. Their use in
  the selected research scenario does not rewrite the official score.

## Known Limitations and v0.4 Handoff

- Canadian evidence coverage requires maintained issuer manifests or a future
  properly licensed regulatory data source.
- Historical runs remain file-based rather than stored in a queryable database.
- Change detection, scheduled weekly comparisons, and a dashboard are deferred
  to v0.4 Platform.
- AI usage requires separately configured provider credentials and remains
  opt-in.
